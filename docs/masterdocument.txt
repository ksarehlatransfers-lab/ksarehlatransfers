# Saudi Arabia Private Transfers
## Master Website Development, Branding, SEO & Content Guidelines

**Project objective:** Build a premium, nationwide private transportation and transfer website covering Saudi Arabia.

**Technology:** Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui.

**Development strategy:** Homepage and brand foundation first, followed by city-based and service-based SEO clusters.

---

# PART 1 — PROJECT VISION

The website must provide private transportation and transfer services throughout Saudi Arabia.

It must not be positioned as a single-city taxi website. It must represent a nationwide private-transfer service.

The website should serve:
- International tourists
- Local residents
- Families
- Business travelers
- Hajj pilgrims
- Umrah pilgrims
- Ziyarat visitors
- Groups and large families
- Corporate and hotel customers
- Travelers needing intercity and cross-border transportation

The website should help visitors understand available services, choose their journey, request a quote, and arrange transportation.

# PART 3 — DEVELOPMENT PHASES

## Phase 1: Homepage and brand foundation

The first development phase is exclusively focused on the homepage.

Before building additional pages, establish:
1. Brand identity
2. Color palette
3. Typography
4. Design tokens
5. Navigation system
6. Footer system
7. Button and CTA styles
8. Form components
9. Image treatment
10. Responsive design rules

The homepage must communicate nationwide service coverage and establish trust.

Do not start generating dozens of city pages before the homepage design system is approved.

## Phase 2: City-based SEO clusters

After the homepage, develop city-by-city clusters.

Potential initial city groups:
- Riyadh
- Jeddah
- Makkah
- Madinah
- Dammam
- Al Khobar
- Taif
- Abha
- Other cities based on actual service availability and keyword research

Each city should have a distinct content and design strategy.

For example:
- Makkah: pilgrimage transportation, airport transfers, hotels and Ziyarat.
- Madinah: Umrah, Ziyarat, airport and intercity transportation.
- Jeddah: airport, hotel, Makkah connections and city transfers.
- Riyadh: airport, corporate, hotel and intercity transportation.

Makkah and Madinah must include relevant Hajj and Umrah coverage.

## Phase 3: Dedicated service clusters

Create separate service-focused sections and landing pages for:
- Hajj transfers
- Umrah transfers
- Ziyarat transfers
- Day trips
- Border transfers
- Airport transfers
- Hotel transfers
- City-to-city transfers

These pages must provide a different purpose and user experience from the city pages.

Avoid creating overlapping pages that compete for the same keyword without a clear search-intent distinction.

## Phase 4: Route and destination expansion

Build individual route pages when there is genuine search demand and operational capability.

Examples:
- Jeddah Airport to Makkah
- Madinah Airport to Hotel
- Riyadh to Jeddah
- Makkah to Madinah
- Jeddah to Taif

Every route page must answer real questions about that particular journey.

# PART 4 — GLOBAL BRANDING AND DESIGN SYSTEM

## Non-negotiable branding rules

Typography and colors must remain consistent throughout the website.

Once approved, the same design tokens must be reused across every page.

Do not independently change fonts or brand colors when designing a new city or service page.

### Typography
Establish:
- Primary font
- Secondary font, if required
- Heading weights
- Body weights
- Font sizes
- Line heights
- Letter spacing
- Responsive typography scale

Typography must be professional, readable, modern and appropriate for a premium transportation company.

Support Arabic typography if an Arabic version is introduced. Arabic font selection must be compatible with the established brand system.

### Color palette

Define:
- Primary brand color
- Secondary brand color
- Accent color
- Background colors
- Surface colors
- Heading color
- Body text color
- Border color
- Success and error colors

All colors must be defined as centralized CSS variables/design tokens.

Never introduce random colors on individual pages.

### Design consistency versus layout diversity

The brand identity must remain consistent, but the actual page layouts must be different.

This is extremely important.

Do not create one page template and replace:
- City name
- Heading
- Paragraphs
- Images

Every page must have its own visual composition, layout, section order and content presentation.

Different pages can use:
- Split-screen hero layouts
- Full-width photographic heroes
- Editorial layouts
- Route-focused layouts
- Image-led storytelling
- Interactive journey planners
- Destination grids
- Timeline sections
- Comparison layouts
- Map-based layouts

The visual approach must be selected according to the page's search intent and subject.

# PART 6 — SEO TITLE RULES

Every indexable page must have a unique SEO title.

### Preferred title structure

Main Keyword + Brand/Domain Name + CTA

Example:

Makkah Private Transfers | Brand | Book Your Ride

The preferred length is approximately 50–60 characters.

### Length rule

- If the title fits naturally within 50–60 characters, include the brand/domain.
- If it becomes too long, remove the brand/domain.
- Retain the main keyword and relevant CTA where natural.
- Never force unnecessary words just to meet a character count.

Examples:

Makkah Airport Transfers | Book Your Ride

Riyadh Private Transfers | Brand | Get a Quote

Madinah Ziyarat Transfers | Reserve Your Driver

Important SEO qualification:

Google does not guarantee that titles will display exactly as written. The 50–60 character target is our editorial guideline, not a strict Google ranking requirement. Search intent, clarity and relevance take priority over mechanically adding a CTA.

Each page must have:
- Unique title
- Unique meta description
- Canonical URL
- Appropriate robots directives
- Open Graph metadata
- Relevant structured data where justified

Do not duplicate titles across city or service pages.

# PART 7 — CTA STRATEGY

Every page must have its own relevant CTA strategy.

CTA examples:
- Book Your Private Ride
- Reserve Your Driver
- Request Your Airport Pickup
- Get a Transfer Quote
- Arrange Your Umrah Journey
- Book Your Ziyarat Tour
- Plan Your Day Trip
- Request Cross-Border Transport

Rules:
1. Do not repeat the exact same CTA section design across all pages.
2. Use wording appropriate to the visitor's intent.
3. Make the CTA visually prominent.
4. Include a clear next action.
5. Use consistent brand button styling.
6. Ensure the CTA works on mobile.
7. Link to the correct inquiry or booking flow.
8. Do not advertise instant booking unless the functionality exists.

The global navigation can have a consistent quote link, but individual page CTA sections must have unique messaging and presentation.

---

# PART 8 — HEADING STRUCTURE

Every page must follow a logical heading hierarchy.

Rules:
- Exactly one H1 per page.
- H2 for major sections.
- H3 for subsections.
- Do not skip heading levels without a structural reason.
- Do not use headings merely for visual styling.
- Do not stuff keywords into headings.
- Headings must explain the content that follows.

The H1 should clearly communicate the main topic and search intent of the page.

The SEO title and H1 should be related but need not be identical.

Decorative text must not accidentally create additional H1 elements.

# PART 9 — INTERNAL LINKING

Internal linking is one of the most important parts of this project.

Every page must participate in a deliberate internal linking structure.

### Homepage
Link to:
- Important destinations
- Major services
- Airport transfers
- Hajj and Umrah services
- Ziyarat services
- Important journey categories

### City pages
Link to:
- Airport transfers in that city
- Hotel transfers
- Relevant nearby destinations
- City-to-city routes
- Relevant pilgrimage services
- Day trips

### Service pages
Link to:
- Relevant city pages
- Relevant route pages
- Related services
- Booking and inquiry pages

### Route pages
Link to:
- Origin city
- Destination city
- Relevant airport page
- Relevant service page
- Alternative related routes

Rules:
- Use descriptive anchor text.
- Avoid generic links such as "click here."
- Avoid excessive linking.
- Do not link unrelated pages simply to increase link counts.
- Use breadcrumbs where appropriate.
- Avoid orphan pages.
- Maintain a logical hierarchy.
- Link to the most relevant conversion page.

Internal links must help visitors and search engines understand the relationship between pages.

# PART 10 — CONTENT QUALITY AND AI SEARCH OPTIMIZATION

The website must be built for both traditional search engines and AI answer engines.

Target discovery platforms include:
- Google
- Bing
- ChatGPT
- Perplexity
- Gemini

The objective is to make the website a reliable, useful and understandable source for private transportation information.

### Content requirements

Every page must:
1. Be original.
2. Provide real value.
3. Answer actual customer questions.
4. Match the visitor's search intent.
5. Explain the service clearly.
6. Include relevant journey information.
7. Explain the booking process.
8. Provide useful transportation details.
9. Avoid unnecessary filler.
10. Demonstrate experience and operational knowledge.

### AI-friendly content structure

Use:
- Clear introductory answers
- Descriptive headings
- Concise explanations
- Useful lists
- Relevant comparison tables
- Step-by-step booking instructions
- Specific service information
- Accurate FAQs
- Clear company/service information

Where appropriate, explain:
- Who the service is for
- Where pickup is available
- Where the journey ends
- What vehicle options are available
- How to request a quote
- What information customers should provide
- What is included and excluded
- Which requirements must be checked before travel

Never invent:
- Customer reviews
- Business registrations
- Licenses
- Fleet ownership
- Insurance coverage
- Prices
- Service availability
- Driver credentials
- Partnerships
- Operational claims

Only publish verifiable business information.

### Structured data

Use appropriate schema where the page genuinely qualifies.

Potential types:
- Organization
- WebSite
- BreadcrumbList
- Service
- FAQPage only when eligible and appropriate under current Google guidelines
- LocalBusiness only when the actual business and location information support it

Structured data must match visible page content.

Schema does not guarantee rich results or AI citations.

Do not create fake local business addresses or misleading location signals.

# PART 11 — IMAGES AND VISUAL ASSETS

Every important page must use relevant, high-quality images.

Image rules:
1. Use real, relevant photographic assets.
2. Prefer Saudi-specific locations and recognizable environments.
3. Use appropriate vehicles and transportation scenes.
4. Select images according to the individual page.
5. Do not reuse the same hero image everywhere.
6. Avoid generic stock imagery when a relevant destination photograph is available.
7. Use properly licensed images.
8. Optimize image file sizes.
9. Use modern image formats where supported.
10. Add meaningful alt text.
11. Use Next.js Image optimization where appropriate.
12. Avoid unnecessary decorative images that slow down the page.

For example:
- Makkah pages should use relevant Makkah imagery.
- Madinah pages should use relevant Madinah imagery.
- Airport pages should show relevant airport/transportation contexts.
- Ziyarat pages should use relevant destination imagery.

Never present a generic image as proof of an actual fleet, vehicle, hotel partnership or service operation.

---

# PART 12 — FAQ AND ACCORDION SYSTEM

Every relevant service and destination page should have a useful FAQ section.

The questions must be expandable.

Requirements:
- Closed by default unless there is a strong UX reason otherwise.
- Click/tap to expand.
- Click/tap again to collapse.
- Keyboard accessible.
- Proper ARIA attributes.
- Clear visual indication of open/closed state.
- Smooth, restrained transitions.
- Mobile-friendly spacing.

Questions must be page-specific.

For example, Makkah airport transfers should not have the same FAQ questions as Riyadh corporate transportation.

Answers must be accurate, helpful and direct.

Do not add FAQs merely to insert more keywords.
