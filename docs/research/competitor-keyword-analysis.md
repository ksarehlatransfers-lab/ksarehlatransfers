# Competitor keyword analysis — taxiserviceksa.com

## Evidence standard used in this document

Every row below is classified by **how strong the evidence is**, because the
specification requires it and because the distinction matters more than the
keyword list itself.

| Label | Means |
| --- | --- |
| **On-page** | The phrase appears in the competitor's title, meta description, H1, H2 or URL. Directly observed on 4 November 2026. |
| **Inferred target** | The page is clearly built for this intent, but the exact phrase is not used verbatim. A judgement, labelled as one. |
| **SERP-observed** | The competitor's URL appeared in a live search result we ran for this phrase. Weak evidence of visibility, not a ranking position. |

**No row in this document is labelled a "ranking keyword."** No rank tracker,
Search Console access or SEO dataset was available in this environment, so
there is no ranking evidence to cite. Any claim that the competitor "ranks" for
a term would be fabricated. See
[sources-and-research-limitations.md](./sources-and-research-limitations.md).

Likewise there are **no volume, difficulty, CPC or traffic figures** anywhere
below.

## Keyword architecture: what they got right and wrong

**Right**

- **One primary intent per URL across `/routes/` and `/distance/`.** The split
  between "I want to book this journey" and "I want to know how far it is" is
  a real intent split, correctly given two page types.
- **Informational pages feed commercial pages.** Every `/distance/` page ends
  in "Related Taxi Routes".
- **FAQ H3s are written as the question a user types.** This is why their pages
  are citable by AI search.
- **Service pages are intent-led, not vehicle-led** — `women-transport`,
  `wheelchair-taxi`, `group-hiace-hire`, `event-transport`, `b2b-solutions`.

**Wrong**

- **Cannibalisation.** Riyadh–Jeddah has four URLs; Riyadh–Dammam has three.
  The standalone `/riyadh-to-jeddah-private-car` (914 words) adds nothing over
  `/routes/riyadh-jeddah/` (2,769 words) and competes with it.
- **Scaled templating.** 80 near-identical `/guides/international/*` pages and
  63 same-skeleton `/distance/*` pages. The distance set is defensible because
  the data genuinely differs per route; the country set is not.
- **Brand-name targeting.** Nine-plus route pages built on third-party company
  names (`baker-hughes-…`, `dhl-…`, `bechtel-…`, `toyota-…`, `carrefour-…`).
- **Arabic treated as a translation layer, not a market.** 6.3% of URLs, one
  page serving the wrong language, no Arabic distance or Riyadh hub.
- **PAA-harvested FAQs** that answer questions no customer asked.

## Keywords worth taking, keywords worth leaving

| Take | Why |
| --- | --- |
| The `<a>-to-<b>` **distance / driving-time** intent | Real, high-volume-looking informational demand on every route; we already answer it inside our route pages with sourced ranges. Needs a decision on whether to split it out. |
| **"What's included / quoted separately"** framing | Answers the price question without stating a price — exactly the shape our constraints need. |
| **Hourly and full-day driver hire** | A genuine service intent we have no page for at all. **Approved scope is hourly and full-day only** — their weekly and monthly packages are *not* approved for us and must not be reproduced. |
