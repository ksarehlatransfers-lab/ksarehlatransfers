# Jeddah — Arabic keyword mapping

Researched **separately** from the English, not translated from it. The two
are different markets with different competitors and, as the Riyadh phase
established, different vocabulary problems.

**No volume, difficulty or CPC figure appears here.** Same reason as always:
no tool, and a fabricated number is worse than a blank cell.

---

## Two vocabulary findings that shape everything below

### 1. `سائق خاص` is polluted by recruitment

An Arabic search for private-driver phrasing around Jeddah and Makkah returned
**mostly job advertisements** — `سائق سيارة خاص` postings on employment
boards, not transfer services. This is the same finding the Riyadh research
made, and it is now confirmed in a second city.

**Consequence:** `سائق خاص` must not be a primary for any Jeddah page. It can
appear in body copy where it reads naturally, but targeting it would put us
in a results set full of job listings.

### 2. The service-led verbs are `نقل خاص`, `توصيل` and `مشوار`

Confirmed again in the Jeddah results. The published cluster already uses all
three in the roles below, and Jeddah should stay consistent with it rather
than invent a second house style:

| Term | Register | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **نقل خاص** | Formal, commercial | Page primaries and titles — the pattern every published page uses |
| **توصيل** | Everyday, very common | Secondaries and body copy; what people actually type |
| **مشوار** | Colloquial | Body copy only; natural in questions, wrong in a title |
| ~~سائق خاص~~ | Ambiguous | **Avoid as a target** — recruitment-dominated |

## Jeddah airport

| Mapped to | Candidates |
| --- | --- |
| **Primary** | نقل خاص من مطار جدة |
| Secondary | توصيل من مطار الملك عبدالعزيز، استقبال مطار جدة، توصيل إلى مطار جدة، من مطار جدة إلى مكة |
| `<h2>` Arrivals | استقبال القادمين، توصيل من صالة الوصول |
| `<h2>` Which terminal | أي صالة في مطار جدة، صالة الحج |
| `<h2>` To Makkah | من مطار جدة إلى فندق في مكة |
| `<h2>` Rail | قطار الحرمين من مطار جدة |

**`مطار الملك عبدالعزيز` and `مطار جدة` are both in live use**, the short form
more so in everyday phrasing. Both belong on the page: the formal name in the
title and first paragraph, the short form in body copy and headings. This
mirrors how the Riyadh airport page handles `مطار الملك خالد` / `مطار الرياض`.

## Jeddah → Makkah

| Mapped to | Candidates |
| --- | --- |
| **Primary** | نقل خاص من جدة إلى مكة المكرمة |
| Secondary | توصيل من جدة إلى مكة، من مطار جدة إلى مكة، المسافة بين جدة ومكة، توصيل للحرم |
| Reverse | من مكة إلى مطار جدة |
| **The access question** | دخول غير المسلمين إلى مكة، تصريح دخول مكة |

**On the access restriction in Arabic.** The English page states it because
the English-language audience includes non-Muslim business and leisure
travellers who may not know. The Arabic audience is overwhelmingly Muslim and
the restriction is common knowledge, so stating it with the same prominence
would read as condescending.

**Recommendation: keep the fact on both pages, but change its weight.**
Prominent, designed callout in English; a short, matter-of-fact line in Arabic
covering what is genuinely useful to that reader — that checkpoints operate on
the road, and that Hajj-season permit checks can apply to Muslims too. This is
localisation, not a different set of facts, and it is the kind of judgement the
Arabic pages have made throughout the cluster.

## Jeddah → Madinah · Taif · Yanbu

| Route | Primary | Secondaries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Madinah | نقل خاص من جدة إلى المدينة المنورة | توصيل جدة المدينة، المسافة بين جدة والمدينة، قطار الحرمين إلى المدينة |
| Taif | نقل خاص من جدة إلى الطائف | توصيل جدة الطائف، طريق الهدا، المسافة بين جدة والطائف |
| Yanbu | نقل خاص من جدة إلى ينبع | توصيل جدة ينبع، المسافة بين جدة وينبع |

Per the [English mapping](./jeddah-keyword-mapping-english.md) these are **hub
sections in this phase, not URLs.** The Arabic candidates are recorded now so
that if a page is earned later, the mapping already exists.

The `المسافة بين …` pattern is worth noting: as in the Riyadh phase, Arabic
distance queries are a real share of route traffic and are answered in the
stat band rather than being ignored.

## Jeddah hotels and railway

| Intent | Primary | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hotels | نقل خاص من فنادق جدة | Section only, as in English |
| Railway | نقل خاص من محطة قطار الحرمين بجدة | Deferred, as in English |

For the railway, the Arabic naming problem is **milder** than Riyadh's:
`قطار الحرمين` is unambiguous and widely known, where Riyadh's station had
five competing names. If a Jeddah rail page is ever built, that is one fewer
obstacle.

## Arabic-specific open questions

1. Whether Jeddah searchers write `مكة` or `مكة المكرمة` in transfer queries.
   The honorific is near-universal in formal writing but often dropped in
   quick search. Our published pages use the full form; worth measuring rather
   than guessing.
2. Whether `توصيل` or `نقل خاص` dominates for Jeddah specifically. The Riyadh
   phase left the same question open and it remains unmeasured. It affects
   headings, not only metadata.
3. Whether the `الحرم` (the Haram) phrasing outperforms `مكة` for the
   airport-to-hotel journey, since the Haram is the actual destination for
   most of that traffic.

All three become answerable from Search Console once the Riyadh pages have
Arabic query data, which is a better instrument than any estimate.
